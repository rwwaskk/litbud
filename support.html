Helpful Details to Include

  • The LitBud app version and build number.
  • Your iOS version and device model.
  • What you were trying to do when the issue happened.
  • Any error message shown in the app.
  • Steps that reproduce the issue, if you know them.

LitBud Pro

LitBud Pro purchases and subscriptions are managed through Apple. If a purchase does not appear in the app, open LitBud settings and use restore purchases. For billing, refund, or subscription cancellation help, use your Apple account subscription settings.

iCloud Backup

LitBud Pro iCloud backup uses your private iCloud account. Make sure iCloud is enabled on your device and that LitBud has iCloud access. If backup or restore fails, include the error message shown in the app when contacting support.
